Flagship Programme

Educator Certification Programme (ECP)

An 8-day intensive flagship programme designed to build a new category of educators who move seamlessly between industry and academia — integrating theory, practice, AI-led pedagogy, and meta-frameworks for multi-disciplinary teaching.

8-Day Intensive 4 Modules Cohort: 25–30 EFMD + IIFR Co-Certified
Apply Now View Curriculum

Programme Overview

The Educator Certification Programme is for professionals and aspiring educators preparing for impactful academic careers. It integrates theory and practice, AI-led pedagogy, and meta-frameworks that enable multi-disciplinary orientation — equipping graduates to teach, mentor, and lead at any management institution with both practitioner authority and academic credibility.

Graduates earn the Pracademic credential, jointly certified by IIFR and the EFMD Global Network, signalling readiness to step into Professor of Practice and other faculty roles envisioned by India's National Education Policy 2020.

Who this programme is for

  • Senior industry professionals and CXOs transitioning into academia
  • Subject-matter experts seeking academic credibility for teaching engagements
  • Faculty members upgrading pedagogy and research capability
  • L&D leaders building academic-grade learning practices in corporate environments

Curriculum & Modules

Four immersive modules over eight intensive days, blending live faculty engagement, applied case work, and AI-enabled pedagogy.

Module 01

Foundations of Pracademic Practice

The role of the Professor of Practice; integrating theory and experience; teaching philosophy and frameworks.

Module 02

AI-Enabled Pedagogy

Designing engaging classes with AI-assisted preparation, case generation, assessment, and student feedback loops.

Module 03

Case Writing & Multi-disciplinary Frameworks

Crafting teaching cases from your own experience; integrating strategy, marketing, finance, and policy lenses.

Module 04

Research-led Teaching & Career Pathways

Connecting teaching to applied research; identifying placement pathways; building your faculty portfolio.

Module structure is indicative. Final curriculum may evolve with each cohort.

What you'll come away with

  • EFMD + IIFR co-certified Pracademic credential
  • Two original teaching cases authored under faculty supervision
  • A complete teaching philosophy and AI-enabled course design template
  • Placement and engagement pathways across IIFR's institutional network

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the ECP intended for?

Senior professionals, CXOs, subject-matter experts, and academics seeking academic credibility and pedagogical capability to teach at top management institutions.

What is the time commitment?

An 8-day intensive in-person residency at the BVB Delhi campus, plus pre-work and post-residency case-writing under faculty mentorship.

Is the programme recognised internationally?

Yes. The ECP is co-certified by IIFR and the EFMD Global Network — a globally recognised benchmark in management development.

How does placement work?

IIFR's institutional network supports placement conversations with Tier-1 management institutions, executive education programmes, and corporate learning units. Placement is supported, not guaranteed.
