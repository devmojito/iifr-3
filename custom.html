What we build with you

Every custom programme is co-designed with your leadership team — faculty development cohorts, executive education for corporate leaders, institutional transformation programmes, and research-led capability builds. Our faculty bring relevance and rigour; your team brings the context and the people. Together we build the learning that moves your institution forward.

How we work

Phase 01

Discovery & Diagnosis

We sit with your leadership to understand strategic priorities, capability gaps, and learning culture.

Phase 02

Programme Design

We co-design the curriculum, faculty mix, format, and outcomes — tailored to your context.

Phase 03

Delivery & Engagement

IIFR faculty deliver the programme alongside your internal champions — in-person, hybrid, or virtual.

Phase 04

Impact & Continuation

We measure outcomes, share insights, and design pathways for the next phase of capability building.

Typical engagements

  • University & institute faculty development — multi-year programmes uplifting teaching, research, and leadership capacity
  • Corporate executive education — cohort programmes for senior leadership, strategy, and innovation
  • Institutional transformation — accompanying schools and universities through structural and pedagogical change
  • Research-led capability builds — programmes anchored in applied research with your sponsored use cases

Who we partner with

  • Universities, business schools, and professional institutes
  • Corporates with senior leadership development priorities
  • Government, regulatory, and policy bodies
  • Foundations and global development organisations
