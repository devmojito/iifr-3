Doctoral Fellowship

Executive Fellow in Management (EFM)

A doctoral-equivalent fellowship for senior practitioners ready to engage with the rigour of academic scholarship — without leaving practice. Turn decades of experience into research that institutions cite, students read, and the world actually uses.

3-Year Fellowship 6 Stages Cohort: 25 BVB + IIFR + EFMD
Apply Now View Stages

Programme Overview

The EFM is designed for CXOs and senior leaders seeking to contribute via applied research, teaching, mentoring, and thought leadership. It is a doctoral-equivalent fellowship — globally co-certified by Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, IIFR, and the EFMD Global Network — that converts decades of expertise into a lasting academic legacy.

EFM is built for the scholar-practitioner: someone who has run businesses, built institutions, or shaped policy, and now wants their understanding to outlive them as research, frameworks, and pedagogy.

Who this fellowship is for

  • Senior executives, CXOs, founders, and policy professionals with 10+ years of leadership experience
  • Industry practitioners who want to engage with academic rigour without abandoning practice
  • Existing Professors of Practice seeking a globally recognised doctoral-equivalent credential
  • Senior consultants, subject-matter experts, and institutional leaders building academic legacy

Fellowship Stages

Six structured stages over three years, supervised by IIFR faculty and an international advisory panel.

Stage 01

Orientation & Research Foundations

Research methods, scholarly writing, ethics, and positioning your contribution to the field.

Stage 02

Discipline Immersion

Deep engagement with your chosen domain — strategy, marketing, operations, policy, leadership, or related fields.

Stage 03

Research Proposal & Faculty Pairing

Refine your research question; matched with primary and co-supervising faculty across IIFR's global network.

Stage 04

Applied Research & Fieldwork

Conduct your applied research with industry access, organisational sponsorship, and academic mentorship.

Stage 05

Thesis Writing & Publication

Synthesise findings into thesis form; develop publishable papers, cases, and frameworks.

Stage 06

Defense, Convocation & Faculty Pathway

Defend the thesis, graduate as Executive Fellow, and step into teaching, advisory, or institution-building roles.

Stage detail is indicative. Cohort-specific milestones are confirmed at admission.

What graduates take away

  • Doctoral-equivalent Executive Fellow credential (BVB + IIFR + EFMD)
  • Published applied research, case studies, and frameworks under your name
  • Faculty supervision and lifelong access to the IIFR scholar-practitioner network
  • Pathways into Professor of Practice, board, advisory, and policy roles

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EFM equivalent to a PhD?

EFM is a doctoral-equivalent fellowship — co-certified by IIFR, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, and the EFMD Global Network — designed specifically for senior practitioners. It mirrors the academic rigour of a PhD while remaining compatible with continued professional practice.

What is the minimum experience required?

A minimum of 10 years of senior leadership experience. An MBA or equivalent is preferred but not mandatory; relevant senior practice experience is the primary entry criterion.

Do I need to leave my job?

No. EFM is designed around continued practice. Most fellows remain in their executive, board, or advisory roles while progressing through the fellowship.

What is the time commitment?

Plan for 3 years of structured engagement — periodic residencies at BVB Delhi, regular faculty supervision, and committed time for fieldwork and writing between residencies.
