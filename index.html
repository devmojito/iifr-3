Three Pathways

Three pathways to transform your impact

Designed for leaders in academia, industry, and research seeking to create lasting influence.

Industry to Academia

For senior professionals and CXOs ready to move from the boardroom to the classroom — without leaving their expertise at the door. The Pracademic credential enables you to teach, mentor, and lead at any management institution, with both practitioner authority and academic credibility.

Academic Faculty Excellence

For faculty who want the credential to match their experience and the framework to make their teaching create impact. Elevate pedagogy, research output, and global credibility with a recognised certification that opens doors.

Applied Research

For senior executives, CXOs, and experts who want their years of experience to outlive them — converted into scholarship that institutions cite, students read, and the world actually uses. Legacy-building for people who still have work to do.

About IIFR

An autonomous academic institute for India's faculty future.

The International Institute for Faculty & Research (IIFR) is an autonomous academic institute established to strengthen faculty capability, research excellence, and academic leadership across India and the Global South.

Designed for the evolving needs of higher education and professional practice, IIFR brings practitioners and academicians together and integrates relevance and rigour to support impactful pedagogy and applied research.
